# Prelude #
[[Flint Toothache]] wandered into town in the early Autumn. Appearing during a town meeting, he ruffled some feathers. As a [[Rat]], he wasn't entirely welcome. However, [[Arthur No-Mane]] reminded the village that all are welcome in [[Elmbridge]]. Additionally, [[Arthur No-Mane]] threatened [[Flint Toothache]] that if he did not behave, there would be consequences.

[[Reginald]] received a letter from a sister in the service of the [[Golden Acorn]]: [[Sister Rosemary]]. She asked that [[Reginald]] come visit her in [[Oakenhall]].

At the start of autumn, [[Arthur No-Mane]] dreamed he was a maple leaf on the wind. He blew across the land at a stone obelisk. This obelisk was carved with a maple tree losing it's leaves and a mouse turning to leaves and blowing away in the wind.\

[[Fleece]] was afflicted with [[Spiral Eyes]], and acting incredibly strangely. [[Timothy Titmouse]] found him in this state and fetched [[Reginald]] and [[Arthur No-Mane]] to help. [[Reginald]] used the power of the [[Golden Acorn]] to take [[Fleece]]'s suffering, but then fell victim to the [[Spiral Eyes]]. [[Reginald]] walked a far distance to the west before [[Arthur No-Mane]] retrieved him and brought him back to his senses.

# Village #
The village saw another influx of refugees rounded up by [[Pascal]], increasing [[Elmbridge]]'s population.

## Projects ##
The building of houses continued this session. The heroes eventually returned with clay from the [[Estuary]] after striking a trade deal with [[Deeproot]] in exchange for wine from [[Elmbridge]]'s vineyards. This allowed them to continue the project.

# Adventure #
[[Reginald]] used the [[Crystal Acorn of Scrying]] to glimpse a vantage to the east: an [[Abandoned Bird Feeder]].
## Abandoned Bird Feeder ##
On their way to the abandoned bird feeder, the heroes ran into a pack of weasels with eye symbols branded into their fur. The heroes attempted to hide under a leaf, but the autumn winds dragged it out of their hands. The weasel's bullied [[Arthur No-Mane]] about his hairlessness and provoked a fight with [[Flint Toothache]]. A tussle ensued, with [[Flint Toothache]] driving the weasels away with a well placed blast of sand to the eyes. 

They interrogated an unlucky weasel before letting him go. They found that he served [[Lampades the All-Knowing]]. 
## Firewasp Hive ##
The heroes journeyed to a hollow burnt tree. Entering the hollow, they found it was a burnt and destroyed home. Inside it, a [[Firewasp]] was eating the charred wood. They captured the creature and tied it up, using it for light as they explored the hollow.

They found a charred bedroom full of papers. [[Arthur No-Mane]] was able to piece together the remains of a map that later led them to the [[Sinkhole]].

Up the stairs they found a smoke filled menagerie of caged bugs. Imprisoned and sedated by smoke, the bugs were a pitiful sight that [[Arthur No-Mane]] could not abide. The heroes carried all of the cages outside and let the bugs go free. One bug, the [[Lazuli Lady Bug]] took an interest in the party briefly but eventually flew away.

In the highest chamber of the hollow, the heroes discovered a nest of [[Firewasp]]s. The walls were cut into hexagonal cubbies with [[Firewasp]] grubs glowing like little embers. A fire burned in the center of the hollow, tended by a [[Salamander]] named [[Fane the Kindler]].  [[Fane the Kindler]] poured four cups of [[Firewasp]] venom for everyone to drink (which none of the heroes truly intended to) and toasted to [[Scamander the Black]]. This caused all hell to break loose. 

[[Flint Toothache]] attempted to spit the [[Firewasp]] venom into [[Fane the Kindler]]'s face, but it backfired; [[Fane the Kindler]] grabbed [[Flint Toothache]] and forced him to drink it, causing the rat pirate to crumble and hallucinate. [[Firewasp]]s came swarming in to sting the heroes, but [[Reginald]] and [[Arthur No-Mane]] were able to keep them at bay. [[Arthur No-Mane]] grabbed [[Fane the Kindler]] and kicked him down the stairs.

Running frantically down away from the [[Firewasp]] swarm, on the second floor [[Arthur No-Mane]] kicked the dazed [[Fane the Kindler]] down a second flight of stairs. Once outside the hollow, [[Arthur No-Mane]] kicked the salamander a third time into a cage that once held the imprisoned bugs.

They returned to [[Elmbridge]], carrying the hallucinating [[Flint Toothache]] and the caged [[Fane the Kindler]]. [[Flint Toothache]] awoke a day later having dreamed of a giant pit surrounded by [[Salamander]]s and stone temples. The salamanders were throwing captured prisoners' into the pit to feed [[Scamander the Black]]. 

From the defeated [[Fane the Kindler]] the heroes confiscated the [[Smokestone of Concealment]] and handed it to [[Flint Toothache]]. 

## Sinkhole ##
On the way to the [[Sinkhole]], the heroes met [[Wheeler]]. [[Flint Toothache]] traded the [[Unyielding Band of Mastery]] for the [[Lady Slippers of the Spider]].

At the sinkhole, they found a gopher-sized hole leading deep down into the earth. At the bottom, they found themselves in the [[Undertunnels]].

Once there, [[Pascal]] was able to sniff out one of their cousins. They found [[Jord Hammerpaw]] out gathering earthworms. [[Jord Hammerpaw]] led them to his home at [[Deeproot]].
