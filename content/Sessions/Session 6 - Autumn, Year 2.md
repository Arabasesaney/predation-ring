## Heroes ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]]
[[Flint Toothache]]
[[Stella the Snuggly]]
# Prelude #
Having settled in in [[Croaking Cove]], the heroes started their journey far from [[Elmbridge]]. A great storm blew in at the start of Autumn, causing all ships to stay moored. They watched a thunderheads coalesce over the [[Wooded Glade]] and unnatural lightning strike down from the heavens repeatedly in the same spot.

## Word from Karasu ##
[[Karasu]] came flying back to bring word of what had happened. [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] now fortified with the power of the [[Pantheon of the Evertree]] summoned forth lightning in an occult ritual. He used it to destroy [[Bramblefort]] and the [[Golden Company]] forces within it. 

[[Karasu]] reported that [[Dantalion the Unseen]] was shocked by this development. As [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] hates [[Dantalion the Unseen]] for stealing his eye, the fox would likely be out for vengeance.
# Adventure #
## Deeproot ##
Intent on tracking down the [[Nautilus Trihelm]], the heroes set sail on the [[Shrews Revenge]]. They interpreted the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]] would lead them to the [[Undertunnels]] so they visited [[Deeproot]] to source information. Seeking an audience with [[Underqueen Aurelia, the Darkstar]] they gained access to the [[Tabletorium]] in [[Cobblekeep]].

In the [[Tabletorium]], [[Flint Toothache]] found references to the [[Tunnel of Silent Dread]]. Using the knowledge the gained a guide they started the journey through the dark [[Undertunnels]].

## Tunnel of Silent Dread ##
Using the [[Lightning in a Bottle]] to light the way, the trio meandered through the tunnels. They forded a fast-flowing river and found the secret entrance to the [[Tunnel of Silent Dread]]. The long sandy tunnel had old barrels and crates stamped with the sigil of [[Bilgerat Bill]]. While making their way through the tunnel, they triggered a poison dart trap and caught the attention of giant ravenous centipedes. With her extreme strength, [[Stella the Snuggly]] tied them into knots and hurled the assailants away.

At the end of the tunnel was cavern with a pristine rum shipment and a treasure chest of gold and silver. In the chest was one of the three pieces of the [[Ancient Compass]]. [[Flint Toothache]] pocketed it and the heroes fled the [[Undertunnels]].

## Trouble at Sea ##
Reembarking on the [[Shrews Revenge]] the heroes sailed the [[Estuary]] out to the coast of [[Salt Wind Dunes]]. There they searched for a [[Cave Beneath the Ocean Spray]].  A storm came blowing in tossing the [[Shrews Revenge]] about in the waves. While they wrestled with the storm, a large fish bit the ship and punctured it. [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Flint Toothache]] got the crew in order to narrowly escape the situation. Battered and soggy they made their way to the shore. 

Unfortunately, while repairing their ship on shore [[Eddy Blacksnout]] caught up to them. His ship appearing on the horizon, the crew prepared for a ground assault. The [[Shrews]] created a defensive ring and held the incursion at bay. [[Arthur No-Mane]], utilizing his oath to defeat [[Pirate King Rankle]], waded under the water to the anchor of [[Eddy Blacksnout]]'s ship. He climbed the anchor and cut the rigging and caused general chaos. Having turned back the [[Sea Rats]], the [[Shrews Revenge]] was pushed out to sea and escape again.

They sailed to a [[Lighthouse]] on the coast where they met [[Finn the Lightkeeper]]. [[Stella the Snuggly]] smoke catnip with him and questioned him about the area. This gave them a lead to the [[Cave Beneath the Ocean Spray]].

## Cave Beneath the Ocean Spray ##
A tidal cave system hid the next location on the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]]: the [[Cave Beneath the Ocean Spray]]. They navigated the cave system in low tide, lighting the way with the [[Lightning in a Bottle]]. They eventually made it to a large cavern housing a monstrous crab. It towered above the heroes; one claw replaced with an anchor and the remnants of a ship mounted on it's back. It appeared to be sleeping.

[[Flint Toothache]] and [[Arthur No-Mane]] attempted to sneak up to sleeping crab, but [[Arthur No-Mane]] stepped on a sea urchin and cried out. The waking crab began to take notice of the intruders and became violent. [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Flint Toothache]] climbed the crab and made it to the ship on it's back. [[Arthur No-Mane]] deflected the crab's anchor-claw with resolute strength as [[Flint Toothache]] investigated the cabin. There he found the Ancient Arrow, the second part of the [[Ancient Compass]]. Arrow in hand, the heroes ran full tilt from the crab and out of the [[Cave Beneath the Ocean Spray]]

# Village #
Returning to [[Elmbridge]] after their adventure, they arrived to find a funeral for [[Dev Ocean]]. His charms were being hung from the [[Memorial Bridge]]. It was reported that he was killed by [[Curiel the Immortal]].

At the end of autumn the town worked to harness the [[Whiskerrun River]], building channels and aqueducts to provide water to cisterns in town.
# Epilogue #
As the first snowflakes of winter fell out of the sky, a geyser of smoke appeared on the horizon. The [[Siege Tortoise]] was arriving to assault [[Elmbridge]].
