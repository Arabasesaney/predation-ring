## Heroes ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]]
[[Flint Toothache]]
# Prelude #
Having finished the houses in the Spring, the villagers enjoyed roofs over their heads. However, the start of summer brought only threats to the village.

# Village #

## Naval Invasion ##
A joint invasion of Frogs and Rats was seen coming down [[Whiskerrun River]] from the watchtower of [[Elmbridge]]. The beavers [[Archie the Architect]] and [[Rudy]] were summoned to throw together an outer barrier to the shore. [[Pond Pirates]] were flung with catapults into the shallow water near [[Elmbridge]] and attempted to storm the village. With the beaver-made barriers the [[Shrews]] holding a line, the frogs were held at bay.

[[Arthur No-Mane]] used the [[Bamboo Shoot of Ascension]] to poke a hole in the invading [[Rat]] ship. However, rats started using the pole to scamper towards the shore. Using surprising strength in his small hairless mouse form, [[Arthur No-Mane]] shook the rats off the pole.

[[Flint Toothache]] and the beaver pioneers swum clandestinely to the rat ship and attempted to bore a hole in it. However, they were caught in nets and pulled aboard. [[Flint Toothache]] came face to face with his nemesis [[Eddy Blacksnout]]. The beavers and [[Flint Toothache]] were thrown in the brig and [[Eddy Blacksnout]] turned the ship around.
# Adventure #

## Flint Captured ##
[[Elmbridge]] having repelled the attackers, [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Alistair]] jumped astride [[Donkus]] and rode after the ship along land. The rat ship eventually arrived in [[Bucktooth Bay]] where [[Flint Toothache]] was summoned from the brig. [[Eddy Blacksnout]] proposed a deal: [[Flint Toothache]] would bring them to the place where the ship he lost sank. There was a message in a bottle lost in the ship and [[Flint Toothache]] would use the diving bell to descend to the bottom of [[Bucktooth Bay]] and retrieve it. Having no other choice, [[Flint Toothache]] agreed.

While at the bottom of [[Bucktooth Bay]], [[Flint Toothache]] swam out to his sank ship. There he found the message in a bottle as well as sword he lost with the ship. Returning to the diving bell he sensed a presence of [[Something Dwelling in Bucktooth Bay]].

[[Arthur No-Mane]], having sworn to bring down [[Pirate King Rankle]] was blessed with inability to drown. Utilizing this, he walked along the bottom of [[Bucktooth Bay]] to and wrapped the anchor of the ship around some rocks to prevent it from sailing away.

While in the diving bell, [[Flint Toothache]] broke open the bottle and read the message. It contained the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]]; a riddle leading to the hidden treasure of [[Bilgerat Bill]]. He stored the map in his cheek pocket. Bursting forth from the waves, [[Flint Toothache]] made his daring escape by spinning the helm and swimming full tilt to shore. The ship, stranded in the water was unable to pursue. [[Flint Toothache]] and [[Arthur No-Mane]] made it away on [[Donkus]] with the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]] in hand.

## Croaking Cove ##
Knowing that [[Croaking Cove]] was the seat of [[Pirate King Rankle]], the pair took [[Flint's Houseboat]] for a sail. They navigated up the [[Whiskerrun River]] through the [[Salt Wind Dunes]] and then east along the [[Estuary]]. Finally they arrived in [[Lily Pad Pond]] as a thick fog began to cover the pond.

They sailed into [[Croaking Cove]] in the night and fog. Avoiding patrolling [[Fishboats]] they clandestinely entered the fortress. Disguising [[Arthur No-Mane]] as a pirate the pair looked the part as they entered the local tavern: [[Ribbet & Gibbet]].  There they met [[Abdul]], a quartermaster in the employ of [[Pirate King Rankle]]. [[Flint Toothache]] used the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]] to get a meeting with the pirate king and [[Abdul]] led them up to the [[Dreadfort]] where the meeting took place.

[[Flint Toothache]] persuaded [[Pirate King Rankle]] to take him as a privateer for the [[Pond Pirates]]. The prisoners current held in gibbets were pressed into his crew. [[Flint Toothache]] agreed to pay out half of the booty he gained during his voyages and to find (and split) the treasure hinted at in the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]]. [[Pirate King Rankle]] also further demanded that the [[Nautilus Trihelm]], which is said to have been held by [[Bilgerat Bill]], would be his if it was found.

One of the prisoners pressed into service was [[Karasu]]. Glad to be out of the gibbets, [[Karasu]] reported to [[Arthur No-Mane]] his news from [[Dantalion the Unseen]]. They had heard that the [[Pantheon of the Evertree]] had thrown their lot in with [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] and felt it was extremely unwise. Once escaped from [[Croaking Cove]], [[Karasu]] planned to fly back to the [[Crow's Nest]] to report to his superior [[Blacktalon Ren]]. 

With their new ship, branded the [[Shrew's Revenge]], the pair took sail towards [[Shipwreck Shoal]].

## Naval Battle with Yazcabal ##
[[Yazcabal, Occult Opossum]] was sailing hot on their heels however. His cultist crew was gaining on them and appeared to be working some occult ritual aboard. [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Flint Toothache]] turned their ship about in an attempt to ram. This turned out to be a disastrous move as the [[Shrew's Revenge]] was broadsided by arrows.

[[Flint Toothache]] and [[Arthur No-Mane]] lead a boarding party over to the assailing ship but ended up in the water. [[Flint Toothache]] was able to swim to the ship, but [[Arthur No-Mane]] sank like a stone. [[Yazcabal, Occult Opossum]] finished his eldritch ritual to summon [[Lorelei the Hungry]] from the depths. The glowing, blood-red eel appeared before the submerged [[Arthur No-Mane]].

[[Flint Toothache]] threw the [[Smokestone of Concealment]] over the rails and blanketed the ship in darkness. Him and the remaining boarding party dispatched the defenders of [[Yazcabal, Occult Opossum]]'s ship. When the smoke cleared only [[Yazcabal, Occult Opossum]] remained.

[[Arthur No-Mane]] barely avoided being swallowed by [[Lorelei the Hungry]]. Grabbing one of her scaled, he road her out of the water and was flung through the air onto the deck of the ship. [[Flint Toothache]] engaged [[Yazcabal, Occult Opossum]] with his sword, cutting a bloody X onto the opossum and hurling him into the water. [[Lorelei the Hungry]] was attracted by the blood and devoured her follower. Appeased for now, the eel swam off.

Shaken, the heroes tied their newly won ship to the [[Shrew's Revenge]] and sailed off. From the ship they gained a fair bit of wealth along side the [[Cricket of Distraction]].

## Shipwreck Shoal ##
Reaching [[Shipwreck Shoal]] they ran aground. Stuck under the blazing sun for several days [[Flint Toothache]] and the [[Shrews]] made repairs to their ship. Meanwhile, [[Arthur No-Mane]] investigated a pristine sunken ship. There was luckily an undamaged wine rack full of expensive vintages they could salvage for wealth. In addition, [[Arthur No-Mane]] found the [[Voluptuous Racoon Statuette]].
# Epilogue #
The party returned to [[Croaking Cove]] to patron the [[Ribbet & Gibbet]] and spend their hard earned treasure. They met [[Wheeler]] there on the island and trading the [[Voluptuous Racoon Statuette]] for [[Lightning in a Bottle]]. They also traded the [[Cricket of Distraction]] for the [[Cloak of Raven Feathers]].

In the morning, [[Arthur No-Mane]] turned his eyes to the north east as the sun rose. A gigantic [[Siege Tortoise]] was making it's way to the Southeast... towards the [[High Grass Meadow]] and [[Elmbridge]].
