[[Arthur No-Mane]]

[[Stella the Snuggly]]
# Prelude #
A harsh winter of privation saw the destruction of [[Elmbridge]]'s vineyard. To make matters worse, through the snow-blanketed [[High Grass Meadow]] came a [[Siege Tortoise]] to lay waste to [[Elmbridge]].

## Siege Tortoise ##
In the days preceding the battle, [[Stella the Snuggly]] pioneered the idea of building a trebuchet to fire snowballs at the [[Siege Tortoise]]. A large bonfire burned in the center of the fort on it's shell; the villagers theorized that without the warmth of the fire the [[Salamander]]s manning the [[Siege Tortoise]] would freeze.

[[Flint Toothache]] led a guerrilla force to tunnel underneath the snow and create a trap for one of the [[Siege Tortoise]]'s legs. They would pin the behemoth in place and pelt it with giant snowballs.

At first light on the morning of the battle, [[Arthur No-Mane]] heartened the villagers with a speech. As smoke from the [[Siege Tortoise]] hovered over the land, the battle began.

[[Flint Toothache]]'s gambit was a double edged sword: the tunnellers immobilized the [[Siege Tortoise]] front leg and impaled it with spears. [[Flint Toothache]] drew forth his legendary blade, recently recovered from the bottom of [[Bucktooth Bay]]: [[Ratfang]]. With it's unbreakable edge he cut the [[Siege Tortoise]] to the bone and halted it's momentum.

However, the smoke from the [[Siege Tortoise]] created mayhem. The newly built trebuchet started pummeling the [[Elmbridge]] guerilla fighters instead of the tortoise. Tunnels collapsed and salamanders rappelled off the tortoise to fight the invaders.

[[Stella the Snuggly]] took things into her own paws. She was packed into a snowball and launched towards the [[Siege Tortoise]] by the trebuchet. Midair, she activated the [[Cloak of the Dragonfly]] and began hurling salamanders off the fortress.

[[Stella the Snuggly]] noticed an opportunity: the bonfire fed a bathhouse with hot water. She fought her way into the bathhouse and jury-rigged an overpressure scenario by the selective crushing of pipes. The ensuing explosion caused water to overwhelm the bonfire.

[[Flint Toothache]], [[Stella the Snuggly]], and the surviving saboteurs watched as the salamanders made a desperate rush at the walls of [[Elmbridge]]; however, the salamanders froze before they reached the wall. [[Arthur No-Mane]] through his spy glass spied [[Fane the Kindler]] lock eyes with him from a distance before disappearing through a sphere of fire. It seemed he would be back.

[[Arthur No-Mane]], taking pity on the [[Siege Tortoise]] that was bridled into service dispatched the field clinic to help the tortoise and heal it's leg. The tortoise was gently helped back to [[Elmbridge]] where it buried itself under the snow and mud to hibernate.

# Adventure #
## The Path to Canopy ##
Still on a quest for the mythical [[Cloudy Chamber in the Skies]], the heroes ventured into the [[Wooded Glade]]. [[Flint Toothache]] postulated that it would be high up in the trees. 

Trudging through the snow of winter, the heroes found themselves stuck in an icy valley after a rain shower. Luckily, [[Flint Toothache]] had the [[Lady Slippers of the Spider]] and [[Spidersilk Rope]]; with this combination he was able to extract his companions from their predicament.

A few days later, they found a [[Hillock Stone Map]] that pointed them in the direction of a [[Treetop Elevator]]. 

Run by enterprising squirrels, the [[Treetop Elevator]] allowed ground dwellers to rise high into the boughs of the trees. Paying for a yearly pass, the heroes entered the elevator alongside commuters and ascended slowly into [[Canopy]].
## Meeting with Blacktalon Ren ##
In [[Canopy]], the heroes found they were being closely watched by tailing crows. Carefully making their way across the entangled branches that comprised the treacherous roads of [[Canopy]], the heroes sought a vantage. It was not long however until they found their path barred by [[Blacktalon Ren]] with a retinue of crows.

[[Blacktalon Ren]] stated that they were in the domain of [[Dantalion the Unseen]] and that they did not appreciate the heroes presence in [[Canopy]]. She interrogated them on their purported allegiance with [[Pirate King Rankle]] and [[Lorelei the Hungry]]; [[Flint Toothache]] countered that they were merely using [[Pirate King Rankle]] to their own ends. [[Arthur No-Mane]] stated they were aligned to [[Elmbridge]] alone and served no Tyrant.

The heroes stated their intention to find the [[Cloudy Chamber in the Skies]] and to seek the last piece of the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]]. [[Blacktalon Ren]] disclosed that it was already in [[Dantalion the Unseen]]'s possession and that you'd need to treat with him to have any hope of obtaining it. After being given assurances of safe passage, the heroes were disarmed, bound, and blindfolded. They were then flown to the mysterious lair of [[Dantalion the Unseen]].
## Dantalion the Unseen ##
They met at night under a full moon. The heroes waited until a shadow darted past the moon. They then heard the disembodied voice of [[Dantalion the Unseen]]. This invisible shade lurked close but could not be detected.

Under instructions from [[Blacktalon Ren]], [[Stella the Snuggly]] and [[Flint Toothache]] bowed in his presence; [[Arthur No-Mane]] did not and incurred [[Dantalion the Unseen]]'s condescension. [[Arthur No-Mane]] stood firm and [[Flint Toothache]] announced to the owl that he was in the presence of a fellow king. [[Dantalion the Unseen]] was amused that the small folk sought to rule themselves.

Negotiations ensued as the haunting presence of the owl shifted about them. [[Dantalion the Unseen]] was willing to part with the last piece of the map in exchange for one request: the destruction of is ancient enemy: [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]. In a flash of insight, [[Flint Toothache]] realized that [[Dantalion the Unseen]] and [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] each held one another's weakness: the owl nullified the fox's ability to predict the future, and the fox could see the owl. With this deal, [[Dantalion the Unseen]] hoped to rid himself of the only threat to his life.

As part of the deal, [[Dantalion the Unseen]] agreed to furnish each warrior from [[Elmbridge]] a [[Cloak of Raven Feathers]] to disrupt [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]'s power. Additionally, [[Blacktalon Ren]] and her retinue would bolster the attack force. They agreed that they would strike at the spring equinox.

The deal done, the heroes were blindfolded and flown back to the [[Treetop Elevator]].
# Village #
Before the end of winter, the heroes gathered a number of much needed building supplies to bolster their town. They visited [[Deeproot]] to buy a load of clay as well as [[Finn the Lightkeeper]] at the [[Lighthouse]] in [[Salt Wind Dunes]] to get a load of salt.

[[Alistair]] was put in charge of training a dedicated militia to protect the town, but the first foray was disastrous. A great number of carapace armaments were lost while practicing a winter river crossing.

[[Arthur No-Mane]] checked on his year-old [[Sacred Maple]] tree. It resembled closer to a 5-year old tree than a 1-year old. By next year it could be fully grown.

# Epilogue #
The end of year two saw many mementos added to the [[Memorial Bridge]]. Still, [[Elmbridge]] stood.
