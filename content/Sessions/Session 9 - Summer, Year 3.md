## Heroes ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]]
[[Stella the Snuggly]]
# Prelude #
Having slain [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] and secured the [[Nautilus Trihelm]] from the lost hoard of [[Bilgerat Bill]], the heroes turn their attention to [[Dantalion the Unseen]].

## Village ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]] sent the [[Shrews]] to aid [[Alistair]] in the training of an [[Elmbridge]] militia force. This time the training goes well, and a cohesive militia begins to form.

[[Jord Hammerpaw]] in his newly minted smithy stokes the flames to craft items of eldritch power. The fragments from the eye of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] are forged into new implements of power. From the shards he crafts the [[Volteomorphic Blade]], [[Oculus of Fossilized Visions]], and the [[Orb of Electroconveyance]].
# Adventure #
## Interrogating Karasu ##
The heroes find [[Karasu]] in a disagreement with a difficult woodpecker who stole something of the crows. [[Stella the Snuggly]] solves the situation by bursting into the nest of the woodpecker, roughing him up, and return [[Karasu]]'s lost possession. 

[[Arthur No-Mane]] bribes [[Karasu]] with a massive fortune to betray his master [[Dantalion the Unseen]]. Unable to resist, [[Karasu]] outlines a secret path to the [[Lair of Dantalion]] through a hollow tree in the [[Wooded Glade]]. It extends all the way up into [[Canopy]] where the lair resides.

## Sir Peridot the Darting Blade #
[[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Stella the Snuggly]] strike out for the [[Lair of Dantalion]]. Finding the secret entrance, the heroes found the way barred by a chivalrous hummingbird knight named [[Peridot the Darting Blade]].

[[Arthur No-Mane]], detecting that the hummingbird was wavering in it's devotion to [[Dantalion the Unseen]], challenged him to a duel. If [[Arthur No-Mane]] won, [[Peridot the Darting Blade]] would serve him instead. If the hummingbird knight won, then [[Arthur No-Mane]] would serve [[Dantalion the Unseen]].

The duel was brutal, but punctuated with many manly compliments to one another's fighting prowess. In the end, [[Arthur No-Mane]] prevailed and the injured [[Peridot the Darting Blade]] swore to serve him.

## Confronting Dantalion ##
 [[Stella the Snuggly]] uses the [[Orb of Electroconveyance]] to teleport to the top of the tree in hopes of surprising the owl. Instead, they find a murder of crow ninjas ready to ambush them. [[Karasu]] had double-crossed them.

An all-out brawl ensued as a dark film began to blacken the daylight. [[Blacktalon Ren]] joined the fray and dueled [[Arthur No-Mane]] across the narrow boughs of [[Canopy]]. Their duel was short and brutal: [[Arthur No-Mane]] struck her down with the [[Volteomorphic Blade]]. Just as the heroes felt they were getting the upper hand, day turned to night and a full moon replaced the sun in the sky. A silhouette passed across the moon and [[Dantalion the Unseen]] joined the fray.

Thanks to the power of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] bestowed upon them by their amber-wrought items, the heroes could see the form of the malevolent barn owl. Despite this, [[Dantalion the Unseen]] swooped in and scooped up [[Arthur No-Mane]] in a powerful talon. The pair flew into the night sky with [[Stella the Snuggly]] pursuing with her [[Cloak of the Dragonfly]].

[[Stella the Snuggly]] was able to knock [[Arthur No-Mane]] out of the talons of [[Dantalion the Unseen]] and began a mid air brawl. [[Arthur No-Mane]] fell back into [[Canopy]] and used a combination of the [[Lightning in a Bottle]] and the [[Mercurial Decanter]] to spotlight and blind [[Dantalion the Unseen]] in the air. [[Stella the Snuggly]] grappled the owl and sent both of them crashing into the earth, outright killing [[Dantalion the Unseen]].

The tyrant went up and dark blue flames, leaving only his talons. A fell, disembodied voice echoed on the winds. It said to the burning owl, "You have failed me."

## Investigating the Dojo ##
The crows and ravens fled. In their wake, the heroes rooted through the abandoned lair. There they found the remnants of [[Dantalion the Unseen]]'s spy network: cubbies full of scrolls sent back and forth between crow agents. Mining it for information, they found the weakness and location of [[Abraxas the Mind-Taker]]; the snake is located in the [[Salt Wind Dunes]] and needs his baleful gaze reflected upon himself.

# Victory Lap #
After throwing a raucous victory party in [[Elmbridge]], the heroes puttered throughout the rest of the season.

## Visiting Oakenhall ##
They were welcomed as heroes in [[Oakenhall]]. They had defeated the two tyrants plaguing the oak tree city, and [[Sorin, the Walnut King]] threw them a triumph. At the feast he presented the heroes with the [[Flaked Knife of Sacrifice]] and the [[Unquiet Ring]]. 

## Mermaid Market ##
The heroes ventured to the [[Mermaid Market]] within the [[Salt Wind Dunes]]. There they found a thriving, seasonal bazaar centered around a statue of a half-fish, half-mouse. They traded for some much-needed gear and visited [[Wheeler]] who has a permanent stall there.

[[Stella the Snuggly]] also met a fisher-frog named [[Bodo]] from [[Dragonfly Den]]. He promised to teach her to fish on stilts should she ever visit.

## Sunken Vessel ##
The heroes followed at tip from a message in a bottle they picked up in the sands while out traveling. It led them to a fully preserved [[Sunken Ship]] buried under the sand. Digging up enough sand to enter the vessel, the heroes descended into the dark. They found an old badger longship, with minor trinkets still within.

However, in the deepest hull of the ship they found [[Shadeborn]] badger zombies huddling in the dark. Once awakened, the [[Shadeborn]] viciously pursued the heroes out of the ship. Using explosives from his bandolier of vials, [[Arthur No-Mane]] set the ship ablaze and collapsed it under the sand.

## Pearl's Oasis ##
On their trek through the desert, they found a small freshwater oasis with an otter mystic named [[Pearl]] residing by it. She held a conch shell to her ear and asked that the heroes listen to it. The heroes said "no thanks" and left in a hurry. 

## Cliffside Monument ##
The heroes visit the [[Cliffside Monument]]: a gigantic statue of a badger lord on a cliffside by the ocean in [[Salt Wind Dunes]]. The statue held a sword in one hand, and a blazing torch in the other. Interested, the heroes investigated and found the hut of the badger tending to the brazier-flame: [[Nikos]].

[[Nikos]] bullied [[Arthur No-Mane]] pretty heavily during their interaction, but also gave them so interesting history: 

>*Long ago a great evil ruled this land. A mighty king of WIND and CHANGE arose to throw out the evil. He gathered the banners of the strongest woodland warriors. Of course, the badgers came to his call; there are no stronger warriors. Together they cast the evil down and ushered in a new era of peace.*
>
>*The cliffside monument stands as a guide for the badger warriors to return when the banners are once again called. Nikos has been putting more wood to the flame as of late; he can feel that the time is once again approaching. The badgers have already begun to row.*

# Epilogue #
The [[Sacred Maple]] has grown to it's full height, and as the summer ends it's leaves begin to show an iridescence of power. With two tyrants defeated and [[Elmbridge]] entering the spotlight, the hopes of the woodland creatures rest upon the heroes.

And so to, does the ire of the tyrants.
