# Introduction #
Snow began to fall upon the land as Year 3 grew towards a close. The refugees of [[Deeproot]] inhabiting [[Elmbridge]] took a toll on the winter stores. However, as the village had been prosperous this year they endured the winter in relative comfort.
## Death of Jord Hammerpaw ##
 It began with a dark omen: [[Jord Hammerpaw]] was working late crafting wonderous items from the marrow-cursed bones of the late [[Lorelei the Hungry]]. While outside the wall at night, [[Curiel the Immortal]] snatched him away. His hammer now hangs from the [[Memorial Bridge]].
## Sir Montebank ##
A singed hare with a perpetually smoking hare made quite an entrance to [[Elmbridge]] that winter. In a gout of flame, [[Sir Montebank]] entered into the village and proclaimed his titles to the air. The [[Shrews]] immediately formed into a fire brigade and doused the hare with water. And so the earthly representative of the [[Radiant Flower]] came to join the fellowship.
## Letter from Habiba ##
[[Reginald]] received a missive by [[Moth Mail]] from [[Habiba]] in [[Spelunkhome]]. In the letter she indicated that a champion of the [[Pantheon of the Evertree]] must be chosen during the covenant on the Spring equinox. With [[Arthur No-Mane]], [[Sir Montebank]], and [[Reginald]] all likely sitting on the pantheon, [[Elmbridge]] will have great sway over it's proceedings.
## Letter from Ophelia ##
The sister of [[Theodore Shimmerwing III]] sent a letter by [[Moth Mail]] to [[Elmbridge]] for her brother. In it she complained of her mother, [[Baroness Victoria Shimmerwing]], had contracted weasels as mercenaries for the [[Barony of Shimmerwing]]. She was very unhappy about it.
## Letter from Abdul ##
[[Abdul]] over in [[Croaking Cove]] sent a letter via [[Moth Mail]] to [[Flint Toothache]]. He said that the [[Dreadfort]] was in chaos and the [[Ribbet & Gibbet]] almost burned down. He asked that [[Flint Toothache]] come to the island and reinstate order as the new pirate king.
## The Tenth Claw ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]] met with [[Underqueen Aurelia, the Darkstar]] to trade blades. Handing over his [[Volteomorphic Blade]], [[Arthur No-Mane]] finally held the terrifying [[Tenth Claw]]: the sword forged from the claw of [[Curiel the Immortal]]. [[Arthur No-Mane]] promised that he would slay [[Curiel the Immortal]] and then they would turn to take back [[Deeproot]] from the clutches of [[Diabolus the Resurrector]].
# Adventure #
## Chimera in the Hedge Maze ##
[[Theodore Shimmerwing III]] attempt to fly over the [[Hedge Maze]] and found that an unnatural gravity pervaded the airs above it. It was quite impossible to fly over it without being bodily sucked into the maze!

[[Stella the Snuggly]] gathered her friends and set forward into the maze. With some help from [[Reginald]] and the [[Golden Acorn]], she navigated the maze to it's center. There she met the [[Chimera]], a lizard-creature with three heads: one crow, one weasel, and one toad.

The crow thirsty for knowledge, the weasel valuables, and the toad... well he just wanted some good conversation. By paying a hefty bribe to the [[Chimera]], [[Stella the Snuggly]] was gifted the legs of a [[Marble Hedgehog Statue]]. Not knowing what it was for, she hauled it out of the [[Hedge Maze]].

## Fulfilling a Promise ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]] led the heroes to the [[Fortress of the Crystal Rat]]. There at the gates he was greeted by [[Pawleader Bartlett]]. Almost two years had past since [[Arthur No-Mane]] had cured the rats of [[Crystalpox]] and they were excited to see him.

The heroes went to meet with [[Pyke Flashtail]]. [[Arthur No-Mane]] produced the [[Tenth Claw]] to show that the time to strike the cat was upon him. [[Pyke Flashtail]] took up his mighty spear and kneeled to be knighted as one of the [[Knights of the Autumn King]].

The [[Ninth Legion]] rats who follow [[Pyke Flashtail]] had been ready to defect from the claws of [[Curiel the Immortal]] for many years. Now, they followed the heroes to [[Catsylvania]] to end the cat's reign.
## A Strange Find ##
As the party traversed the snowy [[High Grass Meadow]], a strange whispering began below the snow. They traced the origin of the whispering to somewhere deep below the frozen ground. [[Sir Montebank]] used his fire magic to melt through the snow and ice. He softened the frozen ground and dug up a chest. Within it was the [[Adderscale Armor]]. Sensing he would need it soon, [[Arthur No-Mane]] donned it.
## Infiltrating Catsylvania ##
[[Pyke Flashtail]] had a contact in [[Catsylvania]] that could him them sneak into the [[Cratecastle]]. His name was [[Vincent]] and he ran the [[Saucer]], a bar and soymilk distillery. [[Flint Toothache]] snuck into [[Catsylvania]] with a small cadre of defected [[Ninth Legion]] to meet with [[Vincent]].

Meeting beneath the [[Saucer]], they hatched a plan to put the heroes in soymilk and catnip barrels. The [[Church of Ninth Life]] required donations every day, and they planned to execute the plan that night.
## Sneaking into Cratecastle ##
As evening fell upon the perpetually gloomy [[Catsylvania]], a caravan of barrels and crates carried by [[Pyke Flashtail]]'s traitor [[Ninth Legion]] rats made their way to the gates of [[Cratecastle]]. The heroes, with the exception of [[Flint Toothache]], held their breath as they were smuggled into the fortress.

[[Cupbearer Oleg]] met the carvan and directed them to the [[Church of Ninth Life]]. The crates and barrels were deposited and locked in a storeroom in the church. Seeing that his friends would need to get broken out, [[Flint Toothache]] snuck back into the church through a window and pickpocketed the keys off of [[Cupbearer Oleg]].

However, in a stroke of clumsiness, [[Arthur No-Mane]] made a racket while extricating himself from his barrel. [[Cupbearer Oleg]] was alerted and started to run for help. [[Theodore Shimmerwing III]] shot him with his hand crossbow and the rat priest began screaming for help. [[Sir Montebank]] got a bit carried away and summoned the powers of the [[Radiant Flower]]. [[Cupbearer Oleg]] combusted into flames and set the entire church on fire. Alarm bells began ringing and all hell broke loose.
## Burning Down the House##
[[Ninth Legion]] rats began swarming from everywhere in [[Cratecastle]]. The heroes, now stuck inside a burning church and surrounded by enemies had to act fast.

[[Arthur No-Mane]] broke through the side of the church and guided his friends out. He used the [[Bamboo Shoot of Ascension]] to shoot his friends upwards into the cat walks high in the rafters of [[Cratecastle]]. Unfortunately, [[Sir Montebank]]'s section of pole burned off and sent him pole-vaulting down to the ground. [[Arthur No-Mane]] dove after him and protected him from a deadly fall, his new [[Adderscale Armor]] absorbing the blow.

[[Ninth Legion]] swarmed up ladders and into the catwalks after the heroes. [[Reginald]] blasted a stormy summer wind at one line of rats coming to get them, sending them tumbling away. [[Stella the Snuggly]] rolled into a ball and crushed the other line of rats coming to intercept them. [[Flint Toothache]] and [[Theodore Shimmerwing III]] rained down bolts and arrows to help the escape of [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Sir Montebank]].

[[Flint Toothache]] let down his [[Spidersilk Rope]] so his friends on the ground could climb up. Instead, [[Ninth Legion]] swarmed the rope and started climbing it.

[[Sir Montebank]] unleashed more wild flames catching a wall of [[Cratecastle]] on fire. The stained glass window depicting [[Curiel the Immortal]] disemboweling a mole began to melt, further increasing the horror of it's visage.

Then, from the darkness of the throne room, [[Curiel the Immortal]] emerged. Her grey fur was mangy and she was crisscrossed with hundreds of scars. Her green eyes were wide with thrill of the hunt.
## Drawing the Claw
[[Curiel the Immortal]] pounced on the heroes, crushing [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Sir Montebank]] underneath her glowing green claws. [[Sir Montebank]] tried to use his fire to burn through the paws of the cat, but she healed as quickly as he burned her.

To dislodge them, [[Stella the Snuggly]] and [[Reginald]] activated the [[Orb of Arcing Conveyance]]. The pair transmuted into a lightning bolt and struck the back of [[Curiel the Immortal]]. From her back, [[Stella the Snuggly]] swung wildly at the cat to distract her. This gave [[Reginald]] enough time to run to [[Arthur No-Mane]]'s side and bless him with the strength of the [[Golden Acorn]].

Seeing that their blows were futile against the regenerating cat, [[Arthur No-Mane]] finally drew forth the [[Tenth Claw]]. It warped the air with it's curse of death. It's sickly green runes glowed brightly. Lit by the flames of the burning church and the shifting colors of the stained glass windows, the fight to the death began in earnest.
## Sheathing the Claw ##
[[Curiel the Immortal]] unleashed her viridian clawbeam: a volley of luminous, spectral blades. Heroes took cover while [[Ninth Legion]] were mangled and decapitated. [[Arthur No-Mane]] countered with the [[Mercurial Decanter]], creating a magical mirror from thin air and reflect the magic bolt. It severed a paw and the cat screamed in pain.

Still, the cat was lightning fast and still the [[Ninth Legion]] closed around to corner the heroes. [[Arthur No-Mane]] struck at the cat with the [[Tenth Claw]], keeping the sword close to nullify her foul regeneration.

[[Stella the Snuggly]] slammed Curiel with her spikey bulk, knocking her over. [[Sir Montebank]] charged in and slashed at the cat with his sword. [[Flint Toothache]] shot a one of his [[Nightwing Arrows]] into her eye. [[Theodore Shimmerwing III]] delivered a bolt of his own into the same eye of the cat, further blinding and infuriating her. 

[[Curiel the Immortal]] regained her feet and leapt into the air. She backhanding [[Theodore Shimmerwing III]] and blasted [[Flint Toothache]] with another viridian clawbeam. Thinking quickly, [[Flint Toothache]] twirled his [[Cloak of Raven Feathers]] and negated the deadly magic.

As the cat came falling back to earth, [[Alistair the Scarred]] boosted [[Arthur No-Mane]]. In a chain of gravity-defying, anime physics, each hero hurled [[Arthur No-Mane]] towards [[Curiel the Immortal]]. His reedy mouse voice roared "ELMBRIDGE!" as the [[Tenth Claw]] slashed through the cat.

She erupted in sickly green flames, burning away to ash. Only her nine remaining claws clattered to the floor. A dark, disembodied voice spoke over her body: "You have failed me."
# Epilogue #
Injured and exhausted, the heroes stumbled from [[Cratecastle]]. The curse of Curiel had been lifted, and the sun finally shown over the town of [[Catsylvania]]. The villagers cheered their liberators and helped them on their way home to [[Elmbridge]].
## Village additions ##
Over the winter the militia trained in archery, the fortifications were further reinforced, and [[Pyke Flashtail]] and his rats joined the village. Additionally, a new trade relation with [[Catsylvania]] opened up and the [[Elmbridge]] began importing soybeans.
