# Prelude #

# Village #
 Winter gripped the land, but with it's ample stores Elmbridge weathered it easily.

## Projects ##
The village added to it's fortifications a tall watchtower. It also added a field clinic to aid the sick and wounded. Lastly, the large root of the elm tree that extends above the town like a bridge (hence it's name [[Elmbridge]]) was made into a memorial. Charms of the deceased are hung from it and hang over the town.

The villagers worked hard through the winter to continuing building houses, but it put a strain on their morale.

# Adventure #
With [[Fleece]] missing, the heroes had no choice but to try and find him. Witnessed by [[Juniper]] on the memorial bridge of [[Elmbridge]], [[Arthur No-Mane]] made a vow to bring the hostage mole home.

## Trip to Deeproot ##
[[Flint Toothache]] hitched his stag beetle [[Donkus]] to a sled made by [[Archie the Architect]] and [[Rudy]]. With it, they plowed through the snow up to [[Deeproot]] in order to gather supplies. [[Flint Toothache]] turned some porcupine quills he found in [[Session 3 - Autumn, Year 1]] into enchanted arrows.

## Rescuing Fleece from Bramblefort ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]] tracked [[Fleece]] far north, crossing over the [[Estuary]] and into the [[Wooded Glade]]. There they found the [[Bramblefort]] under siege by the [[Golden Company]]. The roguish [[Flint Toothache]] did some searching around and found that the fort had a tunnel connecting it to the [[Lair of Lampades]]. They opted to scale the walls rather than go through the lair.

[[Reginald]] used the power of the [[Golden Acorn]] to see the party safely through the thorny moat surrounding the [[Bramblefort]]. However, this caused them to be spotted by the watch and put the fort on alert. [[Flint Toothache]] used the [[Lady Slippers of the Spider]] to climb up the wall and throw down a rope for his companions to come up.

Pandemonium ensued the heroes attempted to break into the keep. [[Arthur No-Mane]] broke his sword trying to cut open the door. [[Reginald]] and [[Arthur No-Mane]] got grappled by weasels then escaped, running circles around the keep. [[Flint Toothache]] shot poisoned arrows into the crowd of weasels. [[Stella the Snuggly]] rolled up into a spiky ball, overtaking weasels and rolling them over. [[Stella the Snuggly]] ceased her rampage by slamming into the keep door and busting it open. An all out brawl in front of the keep ensued. The weasel [[Magnus Slinktail]] attempted to bully the party into surrendering but was unsuccessful.

[[Flint Toothache]] used the [[Smokestone of Concealment]] to cover the battlefield in darkness and slip through into the keep, grabbing [[Reginald]] along the way. Inside the keep they liberated the [[Shrews]] who were kept as slaves. Throwing the ornamental shields on the walls into the hands of the [[Shrews]], they ran on. They looted everything they could in the keep and found [[Fleece]] in a cellar jail cell. They busted him out and he joined in the wild looting of [[Bramblefort]].

While in the royal apartments of the keep, [[Reginald]] locked eyes with a pure white ferret escaping. She would later turn out to be [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]].

Out in the courtyard, the darkness from the [[Smokestone of Concealment]] cleared to reveal a new challenger: [[Thane Ivar Kill-Coney]]. Hefting his massive axe Rabbitsbane, he entered the fray. [[Arthur No-Mane]] challenged him to a one-on-one combat which he accepted. The remaining weasels and [[Stella the Snuggly]] formed a shield wall around the combat. 

[[Thane Ivar Kill-Coney]] was disarmed by [[Arthur No-Mane]], but began pummeling the mouse with his powerful fists anyways. [[Arthur No-Mane]] was then likewise disarmed and put on a the back paw. [[Stella the Snuggly]] spotted a weak spot in the long underside of the weasel and called out "He's got a jelly-belly!" [[Arthur No-Mane]] sucker-punched the massive weasel and gained the upper hand. Slipping in the churned up mud, the larger weasel began to tire. The small and quick [[Arthur No-Mane]] dodged and parried until [[Thane Ivar Kill-Coney]] collapsed exhausted. The remaining weasels broke and ran.

The party with [[Fleece]] and the [[Shrews]] in tow slid down a rope over the side of the wall to escape into the forest. The last thing [[Arthur No-Mane]] saw was the [[Golden Company]] assaulting the [[Bramblefort]]. With no weasels left to defend it, they were sure to take it.
## Pantheon of the Evertree ##
Late in winter [[Sister Rosemary]] came knocking at [[Reginald]]'s front door. She had a letter summoning him to the [[Pantheon of the Evertree]]. The red squirrel traveled to the [[Hollow of the Pantheon]] in the [[Wooded Glade]] to speak on the council.

He found that council divided and planning to vote on which Tyrant to support. [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]] argued that this would be better than all eight [[Tyrants]] causing war and strife between each other; a quick end to the war would be better than a prolonged conflict. [[Reginald]] argued in vain that this was folly, but [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]] outfoxed him. The [[Pantheon of the Evertree]] voted to support [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] as the chosen Tyrant.
