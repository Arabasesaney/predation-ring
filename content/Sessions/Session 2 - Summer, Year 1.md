## Heroes ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]]
[[Reginald]]

# Prelude #
Wandering into [[Elmbridge]] came [[Reginald]], red squirrel cleric of the [[Golden Acorn]]. Sworn to help those in need, he now helps guide the village of [[Elmbridge]].

The end of spring saw the funeral of [[Methusael]]. During the service, [[Reginald]] buried a sacred acorn with him. 
# Village #
The summer saw many new villagers make their home in [[Elmbridge]]. [[Pascal]] returned with more wandering refugees and a beaver family settled in on the stream fed from the [[Mists of Wonder]].  

## Villagers ##
The beaver family formally joined the village: [[Archie the Architect]] & [[Bethette]] along with their many children.
## Projects ##
Having completed the grass woven palisade and finished planting fields, the residents of [[Elmbridge]] turned their attention to building houses. With the help of [[Archie the Architect]], they began picking up stray wood by the elm tree and turning them into planks.
## Raid by Golden Company ##
A troupe of robins lead by [[Featherlord Brutus]] of the [[Golden Company]] attempted to raid the village. They were successfully routed by the sword of [[Arthur No-Mane]] and divine powers of [[Reginald]].

# Adventure #

## Erratic Boulder ##
On the trip through the tall grasses of [[High Grass Meadow]], [[Arthur No-Mane]] noticed a crow spying on them. He and [[Reginald]] ambushed the crow and questioned him fiercely, rubbing his beak into the dirt until he squawked. 

The crows name was [[Karasu]] and was a spy for [[Dantalion the Deathwing]]. [[Arthur No-Mane]] plucked a feather from the crow and threatened him with curses if he didn't cooperate. [[Karasu]], terrified, agreed to be a double agent for [[Elmbridge]]. Satisfied with the outcome, the pair let [[Karasu]] go and headed to the boulder.

Using the [[Bamboo Shoot of Ascension]], the heroes climbed to the top with no issue. In the distance, they spotted several new points of interest to add to the map.
## Vernal Troll ##
The heroes planned to visit a large [[Anthill]] to the west. However, to get there they needed to cross the path of the [[Vernal Troll]]. The bridge over a vernal pool was guarded by a great bullfrog. On the way through, they paid the troll 1 wealth a piece.

## Anthill ##
Upon reaching the [[Anthill]], the heroes hatched a plan. By playing dead, the heroes allowed themselves to be dragged inside the anthill by worker ants. Once inside they rooted around in the storage chambers. Surprisingly, they found and object of great power: the [[Crystal Acorn of Scrying]]. Reginald scooped this up, assuming it to be a holy tool of the [[Golden Acorn]].

They snuck into the queen's chamber and attempted to ambush her in hopes of scattering her legion. What ensued was a desperate brawl in the darkness of the cavern. The combined might of [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Reginald]] was narrowly enough to slay the queen and scatter her warriors. The [[Anthill]] now empty of protectors, the heroes planned on how to get the ant's winter stores home.

## Vernal Troll Part 2 ##
Wanting to avoid the toll, [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Reginald]] snuck past the [[Vernal Troll]]. However, Arthur managed to forget his sword on the wrong side. When he attempted to stealthily retrieve it, his foot fell through the bridge and provoked the bullfrog. He was pulled into the murky waters and half drowned before narrowly escaping.

## Getting the Goods Home ##
Once returned to [[Elmbridge]], the villagers loaded up pack beetles with carts to journey to the now abandoned [[Anthill]]. Having to return through the [[Vernal Troll]], [[Arthur No-Mane]] brokered a deal with the bullfrog; he would provide the bullfrog with ants (including the rare queen ant) as payment for their crossing. The bargain was struck; the villagers cleared out the [[Anthill]]'s storage and brought the hoard of food and supplies home to [[Elmbridge]].

# Epilogue #
A grand celebration was held in [[Elmbridge]]. The food from the [[Anthill]] heavily bolstered the winter stocks and the wagonloads of ant carapace were fashioned into armaments to protect the town. The addition of a vineyard to the agriculture fields provided wine for the festivities. Foundations for future homes were laid that summer. And upon those foundations the residents of [[Elmbridge]] danced.

However, as the weather cooled and the autumn winds blew in so did a strange visitor. [[Timothy Titmouse]] was the first to witness a villager behaving strangely: a mole with spiral eyes.
