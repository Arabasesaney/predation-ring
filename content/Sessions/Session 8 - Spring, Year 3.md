## Heroes ##
[[Arthur No-Mane]]
[[Flint Toothache]]
# Prelude #
Having struck a deal with [[Dantalion the Unseen]], the heroes planned to slay [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] in exchange for the last piece of the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]]. On the spring equinox, they would strike.

# Adventure #

## Lair of Lampades ##
The heroes armed up and departed for the [[Wooded Glade]]. The [[Shrews]] and a number of [[Elmbridge]] warriors joined the heroes in a warband. [[Blacktalon Ren]] and her entourage of crows met the heroes at the smoking remains of [[Bramblefort]]. From there, they entered the lair of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]].

They found an eldritch laboratory within the dark foxhole. It was lit by electric lights and wires spooled across the ground. Giant tomes of knowledge were littered everywhere. Mad scrawling covered every surface.

In the laboratory, [[Flint Toothache]] found the [[Mercurial Decanter]].

The heroes found the bodies of woodland creature encased in amber. They were being used as batteries to power the "Accumulator", an eldritch device capable of summoning bolts of lightning. Hoping to destroy the device, [[Arthur No-Mane]] unleashed his [[Lightning in a Bottle]] into the electrical grid. This caused the system to overload, bursting amber pods and electrical lights. In the darkness, the giant glowing amber eye of Lampades opened.

## Fighting Lampades ##
Secret doors opened in the burrow and weasels began pouring out. The warriors of [[Elmbridge]] and the crows of [[Blacktalon Ren]] cried out in surprise and fought furiously for their lives. The giant fox began to swipe its great paws and unleash lightning upon it's foes.

With [[Arthur No-Mane]] defending him, [[Flint Toothache]] used the [[Spidersilk Rope]] to wrap the legs of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]. A swipe of the paw sent [[Arthur No-Mane]] flying, but [[Flint Toothache]] was able to bring the tyrant down to the ground. [[Flint Toothache]] began unleashing arrows into the eye of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]], cracking it.

[[Arthur No-Mane]] was intercepted by [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]]; her allegiance with [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] made clear. They dueled, [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]] attempting to protect her master. 

[[Flint Toothache]] tried to pour the [[Mercurial Decanter]] into the cracks of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]'s amber eye, but the fox blasted it with a laser beam of lightning. [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]] were caught in the blast; it singed [[Arthur No-Mane]] and disintegrated [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]].

In a final stroke, [[Flint Toothache]] drove his vorpal sword [[Ratfang]] into the eye of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]. With a horrible scream, the amber eye shattered and the fox became a conflagration of yellow flame. The weasels routed and the warriors of [[Elmbridge]] cheered. All that was left of the cunning fox was the pieces of his eldritch eye.

## Crossing the Estuary ##
When crossing the [[Estuary]], the heroes caught sight of [[Eddy Blacksnout]] blockading the bridge. [[Arthur No-Mane]] attempted to disguise himself as he crossed, failed, and then ran away. [[Eddy Blacksnout]] swam stealthily while [[Arthur No-Mane]] attracted all of the attention.

## Meeting with Dantalion ##
Having gained the comradery of [[Blacktalon Ren]], she and her crows escorted the heroes to their master [[Dantalion the Unseen]]. Meeting once again, [[Dantalion the Unseen]] held up his end of the bargain and bestowed the compass body to complete the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]].

He asked the heroes if they would serve him, but [[Arthur No-Mane]] flatly refused. [[Dantalion the Unseen]] was disappointed by this but did not quarrel. He left them leave in peace.

# Village #
The party returned victoriously. Many days of ceremony and celebration ensued. The warriors who lost their lives were remembered on the [[Memorial Bridge]]. Word traveled quickly about the heroes who slew a tyrant.

[[Alistair]] continued struggling to cobble together a disciplined militia. After two seasons, however, it was not going well.

## Visiting Oakenhall ##
Needing iron to complete the [[Elmbridge]] smithy for [[Jord Hammerpaw]], the heroes trekked across the [[High Grass Meadow]]. They had heard from a squirrel at the [[Treetop Elevator]] that [[Oakenhall]] was the center of civilization and had much to offer.

They found a giant oak tree bustling with activity. The whole tree was a frothing market of squirrels and chipmunks. The heroes were identified quickly and hurried to meet [[Sorin, the Walnut King]]. They met in the throne room that was bursting with acorns, and then had tea in a side chamber.

[[Sorin, the Walnut King]] thanked them for defeating one of their chief adversaries, and offered them a boon. They king promised them a cart of iron and favorable trade in honor of their deed. The king informed them of his other major adversary: [[Dantalion the Unseen]]. The owl is forced the squirrels out of canopy; gathering nuts becomes dangerous work.

The heroes did some trading while in the city, and then took their haul back to [[Elmbridge]] to finish the smithy. 

# Adventure II #
Combining the three pieces of the [[Map of Bilgerat Bill]] they finally held the [[Ancient Compass]]. The compass needle spun vigorously, then stopped on a point in the distance. Hopping aboard the [[Shrews Revenge]] they departed following the compass. It lead them to [[Lily Pad Pond]].

## Treasure of Bilgerat Bill ##
The compass lead them to a sheer cliff island in the middle of [[Lily Pad Pond]]. Touching down on the rocky shore, they searched the barren island and found nothing.

[[Arthur No-Mane]], still able to breathe under water due to his oath to defeat [[Pirate King Rankle]], submerged under water to explore the cliff side. There he found a large opening leading to a hidden cavity inside the island. [[Flint Toothache]] swam through with him and they emerged inside the island. The had found the [[Hideout of Bilgerat Bill]].

There they found the abandoned stronghold of [[Bilgerat Bill]]. This full concealed cavern had a dock with [[Bilgerat Bill]]'s famous shipped moored at it: the [[Vermintide]].

Exploring the cavern they found buildings of limestone still standing in pristine condition. They entered a tavern called the Saltlick. Inside were the skeletal remains of pirates. It seemed like they had all died during a raucous party. [[Flint Toothache]] immediately determined out the cause: all of the wine was poisoned. [[Bilgerat Bill]] poisoned his crew when he went to the afterlife; that way, no one would find his treasure.

The heroes found the mansion of [[Bilgerat Bill]]. Within the throne room, the corpse of the great pirate sat on a gilded chair. In his hand he held the [[Poisonwine Chalice]]. On his head was the famed [[Nautilus Trihelm]]. Covering the floor was a mix of poisoned pirates and copious treasures. Giddy, the heroes packed up their hard-won treasures.

Placing the [[Nautilus Trihelm]] on his head, [[Flint Toothache]] felt the power of the sea rush in. With it, he could empower his ship and crew to swim and sail beneath the waves like a fish. He used the power of the helm to sail the [[Vermintide]] under water, out of the hidden cave, and back to the surface.

## Recruiting Eddy Blacksnout ##
The next step of the heroes plan was to take the fight to [[Lorelei the Hungry]]. As the patron of [[Pirate King Rankle]], they hoped by toppling her it would strip him of his power. [[Flint Toothache]] sailed his new fleet to find his old rival [[Eddy Blacksnout]] in hopes of turning him.

They met on the deck of the [[Vermintide]], and [[Eddy Blacksnout]] was suspicious but also deeply envious of [[Flint Toothache]]'s new found wealth. Knowing greed to be [[Eddy Blacksnout]]'s greatest motivation, [[Flint Toothache]] gave him half the treasure of [[Bilgerat Bill]] to buy his loyalty. With that, the fleet was bolstered further.

The heroes planned to use the power of the [[Nautilus Trihelm]] to submerge the fleet and take the fight to the vicious eel, [[Lorelei the Hungry]].

# Epilogue #
Flush with success and riches, the heroes returned to [[Elmbridge]] to celebrate. They also would need to prepare to topple the next tyrant.
