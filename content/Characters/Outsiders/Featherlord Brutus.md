A blue jay in the [[Golden Company]] leading a band of robins. He lead an unsuccessful raid on [[Elmbridge]] in [[Session 2 - Summer, Year 1]].

He returned again to raid the village under the command of [[Talonlord Braxas]] in [[Session 10 - Autumn, Year 3]]. During this raid he met his end when [[Stella the Snuggly]] used the [[Orb of Arcing Conveyance]] to deep strike the command tent.
