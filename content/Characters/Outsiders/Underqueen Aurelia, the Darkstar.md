The underqueen of [[Deeproot]]. She is a strong, battle-hardened young [[Mole]] who recently ascended the Quartz Throne. She wields the [[Tenth Claw]], passed down to her through generations of monarchs.

She made a deal with [[Arthur No-Mane]] to help rid the land of [[Curiel the Immortal]] if he first helped her with one of the [[Tyrants]] plaguing her city.
