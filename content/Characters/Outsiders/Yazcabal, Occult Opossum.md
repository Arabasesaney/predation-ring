Deceased.

A heretically opossum in a large pope hat. He sports the white circle and black dot of his patron deity. He was first found inhabiting the [[Burrow of the Drowned Rat]] in [[Session 1 - Spring, Year 1]], before he and his ilk were driven out.

In the first encounter with him, het slip his connection to one of the [[Tyrants]] named [[Lorelei the Hungry]].

He was later encountered sailing [[Lily Pad Pond]] in [[Session 5 - Summer, Year 2]]. His attempt to capture the heroes and sacrifice them to [[Lorelei the Hungry]] backfired. Instead, he became a victim of her hunger and was devoured by the eel.
