A company of disciplined and well armed birds in the employ of [[Malphas the Golden]]. They fight for wealth and honor.

They have assaulted [[Elmbridge]] several times under the commands of both [[Featherlord Brutus]] and [[Talonlord Braxas]]. They also sieged and took [[Bramblefort]] before [[Lampades the All-Seeing]] destroyed it with eldritch lightning.
