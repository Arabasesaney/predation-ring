Frogs from [[Lily Pad Pond]] that raid for treasure. They served [[Pirate King Rankle]] before he was slain and sail [[Fishboats]]. Their seat of power is [[Croaking Cove]]. They used to raid [[Deeproot]] viciously.

After the defeat of [[Lorelei the Hungry]], they and the [[Sea Rats]] became leaderless and fell into (more) vice.
