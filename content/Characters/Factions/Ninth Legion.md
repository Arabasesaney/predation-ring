The fanatical and brutal servants of [[Curiel the Immortal]]. They are rats that have clipped their ears into triangular, cat-like shapes and bear tattoos of the Nine-Interlocking-Triangles. Their love of violence, and their fear of Curiel, keep them loyal to the cat.

[[Pyke Flashtail]] and the [[Fortress of the Crystal Rat]] are [[Ninth Legion]], but have wanted to defect for many years.

When [[Curiel the Immortal]] was slain in [[Session 11 - Winter, Year 3]], the Ninth were scattered and disbanded.
