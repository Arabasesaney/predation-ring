A chivalrous and upstanding hummingbird knight formerly in the service of [[Dantalion the Unseen]]. He was defeated in a bout of single combat by [[Arthur No-Mane]] in [[Session 9 - Summer, Year 3]]. After which, he swore to serve [[Arthur No-Mane]].

He later revealed that he squired for a knight who was a member of the [[Knights of the Autumn King]] many years ago.

He now resides in [[Elmbridge]] and serves alongside [[Alistair]].
