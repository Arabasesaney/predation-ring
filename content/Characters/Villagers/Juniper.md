A young mouse and granddaughter of [[Methusael]]. After grieving the loss of her grandfather, she has taken up training with the spear. She wanted to fight back against [[Curiel the Immortal]].

Unfortunately in [[Session 4b - Spring, Year 2]] she was deceived by [[Fane the Kindler]], who offered her the power of fire while captured in [[Elmbridge]]. He manipulated her into setting him free and setting the town ablaze. [[Arthur No-Mane]] attempted to save her, but failed.

In memory of her, the village started [[Juniper Fest]].
