A [[Mole]] smith from [[Deeproot]]. The heroes met him in [[Session 3 - Autumn, Year 1]] while in the [[Undertunnels]]. They convinced him to come back to [[Elmbridge]] to set up shop.

He crafted many wonderous items such as the [[Volteomorphic Blade]], [[Darkmoon Cloak]], and the [[Ossified Armor]].

He was devoured by [[Curiel the Immortal]] in [[Session 11 - Winter, Year 3]].
