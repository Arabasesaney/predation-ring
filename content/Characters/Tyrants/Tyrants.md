The Tyrants make up the [[Ring of Predation]]. These fearsome gods must be toppled to bring peace to the land.

They include:
- [[Abraxas the Mind-Taker]]
- [[Curiel the Immortal]]
- [[Dantalion the Unseen]]
- [[Diabolus the Resurrector]]
- [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]
- [[Lorelei the Hungry]]
- [[Malphas the Golden]]
- [[Scamander the Black]]
