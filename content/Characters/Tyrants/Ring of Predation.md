A dark pantheon of fearsome gods. Known as [[Tyrants]], they rule the land with merciless savagery.

It's members include:
- [[Abraxas the Mind-Taker]]
- [[Curiel the Immortal]]
- [[Dantalion the Unseen]]
- [[Diabolus the Resurrector]]
- [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]
- [[Lorelei the Hungry]]
- [[Malphas the Golden]]
- [[Scamander the Black]]
