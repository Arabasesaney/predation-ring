A fox whose minions are weasels and stoats. He had several named lieutenants such as [[Freya, Chosen of Winter]], [[Thane Ivar Kill-Coney]], and [[Magnus Slinktail]]. They control the power of foreseeing and lightning.

They built an eldritch machine to smite his foes with lightning from extreme distances. Lampades hated [[Dantalion the Unseen]] for plucking out his eye in a fight. However, Lampades replaced it with one of amber and heightened his own powers of foretelling.

They were defeated in [[Session 8 - Spring, Year 3]] by [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Flint Toothache]].
