One of the [[Tyrants]]. Known as the "Silent Death", he cannot be seen by normal means. He controlled a vast spy network of crows and ravens, with [[Blacktalon Ren]] serving as his lieutenant.

He made a deal with [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Stella the Snuggly]] to unseat [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]. He supplied a cadre of his crow shinobi to assault the fox's lair. Having killed his main rival, the owl thought he would be invincible. 

However, he was defeated by [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Stella the Snuggly]] in [[Session 9 - Summer, Year 3]].
