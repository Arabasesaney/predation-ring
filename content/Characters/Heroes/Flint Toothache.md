A rat pirate who showed up to [[Elmbridge]] wet and bedraggled. He was part of a pirate crew that sailed inland to [[Bucktooth Bay]], where they got embroiled in a naval battle near the [[Beaver Dam Fortress]]. 

Flint has a chipped tooth, gold hoop earrings, and a cocky attitude. He yearns to have a ship of his own and outdo his rival [[Eddy Blacksnout]].

He first appeared in [[Session 3 - Autumn, Year 1]].
