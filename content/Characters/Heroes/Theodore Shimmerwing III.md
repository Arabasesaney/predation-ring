A chickadee magician and chronicler from [[Silverbirch Glen]]. He is the fourteenth child of [[Baroness Victoria Shimmerwing]].

He was sent south to the [[High Grass Meadow]] on a diplomatic mission to open trade relations between [[Silverbirch Glen]] and [[Elmbridge]].

He first appears in [[Session 10 - Autumn, Year 3]] and suffered a near fatal blow while fighting [[Lorelei the Hungry]].
