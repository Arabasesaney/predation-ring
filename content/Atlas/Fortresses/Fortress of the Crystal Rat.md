A fortress first encountered in [[Session 1 - Spring, Year 1]]. It is located in the [[High Grass Meadow]].

The [[Rats]] there were afflicted by [[Crystalpox]] when first found. [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Pascal]] uncovered the source of the illness and cured the band of rats.

It is held by [[Pyke Flashtail]] and his lieutenant [[Pawleader Bartlett]]. Unwillingly, they serve [[Curiel the Immortal]].
