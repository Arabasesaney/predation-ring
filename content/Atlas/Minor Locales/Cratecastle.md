The once seat of [[Curiel the Immortal]]. A large wooden crate once built by the [[Giants]]. It has been carved into gothic style and has giant stained glass windows on each of it's four walls.

There are four scenes rendered in blues and reds with excessive bloodiness:
	1) Curiel biting the head of a mouse
	2) Curiel pouncing onto a squirrel, spraying the ground in blood
	3) Curiel disemboweling a mole with a swipe of her claw
	4) Curiel ripping the wing off a bird in mid air

The [[Church of Ninth Life]] resided there.

Cratecastle was burned down when [[Curiel the Immortal]] was felled in [[Session 11 - Winter, Year 3]].
