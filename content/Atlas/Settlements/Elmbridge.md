A budding settlement in the [[High Grass Meadow]] just north of the [[Ashlands]]. It's fertile land is watered by the [[Mists of Wonder]].

A large root from the Elm tree creates a great bridge over the village; it is known as the [[Memorial Bridge]].

It currently has just over a dozen villagers. It is defended by a woven grass palisade and has several plots of new farmland. It has a watchtower, field clinic, and vineyard. It has been importing [[Estuary]] clay from [[Deeproot]].
