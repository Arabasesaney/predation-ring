The city of the moles. Hidden in the [[Undertunnels]] in a large cavity beneath a large elm tree. The roots of the tree dangle down from the ceiling of the cavity. Phosphorescent plants growing on the roots light the city from above.

The buildings are built exclusively with pebbles and clay from the [[Estuary]]. In the center, Cobblekeep casts a shadow over the Deeproot. It is the seat of [[Underqueen Aurelia, the Darkstar]].

The moles here are expert crafters, mining ore in the [[Undertunnels]] to forge exquisite metal tools and ornaments.

The city has been beset by [[Pond Pirates]] on the [[Estuary]]. Additionally, the unsettling minions of [[Abraxas the Mind-Taker]] and [[Diabolus the Resurrector]] have been plaguing the city.
