A large oak tree settlement full of squirrels. It's located in the [[High Grass Meadow]].

It was first visited by the heroes in [[Session 8 - Spring, Year 3]], where they were welcomed by it's ruler [[Sorin, the Walnut King]].

Oakenhall has a bustling nut-based economy, and trade many exotic and useful goods in the great oak. There is a nut-stock exchange where squirrels furiously buy and sell different types of nuts.
