A gigantic statue of a badger lord on the coast of [[Salt Wind Dunes]]. It sits positioned on a cliff. The badger lord holds a sword in one hand, and a blazing torch in the other. It is tended to by [[Nikos]]. It was built to ensure that the badger warriors of old would know where to sail when the [[Autumn King]] called upon them again.

In the words of [[Nikos]]:
>*Long ago a great evil ruled this land. A mighty king of WIND and CHANGE arose to throw out the evil. He gathered the banners of the strongest woodland warriors. Of course, the badgers came to his call; there are no stronger warriors. Together they cast the evil down and ushered in a new era of peace.*
>
>*The cliffside monument stands as a guide for the badger warriors to return when the banners are once again called. Nikos has been putting more wood to the flame as of late; he can feel that the time is once again approaching. The badgers have already begun to row.*
