A bridge over a vernal pool guarded by a great bullfrog. The surly bullfrog extracted 1 Wealth from any member going over it.

[[Arthur No-Mane]] got into a tussle with the bullfrog while trying to sneak across the bridge in [[Session 2 - Summer, Year 1]]. Later, he paid his way with the body of the dead queen ant from the [[Anthill]].
