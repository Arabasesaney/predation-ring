A sprawling maze on the outskirts of [[Elmbridge]]. It appeared overnight from a small hedge planted by [[Stella the Snuggly]]. Perhaps the "miracle grow" bought from the [[Mermaid Market]] had something to do with it?

At the entrance to the maze is a stone sign that reads:

> HARK HEDGEHOG
> HEED THE CHANGING HEAVENS
> HASTEN TO THE HEART HEREIN
> HARVEST THEIR HOARDS
> AND EMERGE
> THE HERO OF HAWTHORNE
