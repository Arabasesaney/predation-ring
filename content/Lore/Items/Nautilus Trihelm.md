A treasure lusted for by [[Pirate King Rankle]] and said to be hidden in the hoard of [[Bilgerat Bill]].

It was taken from the corpse of [[Bilgerat Bill]] in [[Session 8 - Spring, Year 3]] by [[Arthur No-Mane]] and [[Flint Toothache]]. 

The helm has the power to submerge ships and crew under the waves. While under the helm's influence, creatures and ships can move and breathe like fishes in the water.
