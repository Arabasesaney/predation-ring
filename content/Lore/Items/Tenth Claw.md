A legendary sword said to be forged from the tenth claw of [[Curiel the Immortal]]. Carved with runes of reversal, [[Curiel the Immortal]]'s healing powers are twisted into a cursed blade of death and decay.

[[Pyke Flashtail]] theorized that if this weapon truly does exist, it is the only thing that can kill [[Curiel the Immortal]].

It is held by [[Underqueen Aurelia, the Darkstar]].

In [[Session 11 - Winter, Year 3]], [[Arthur No-Mane]] borrowed the sword from [[Underqueen Aurelia, the Darkstar]] and used it to slay [[Curiel the Immortal]].
