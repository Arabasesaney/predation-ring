This burnished copper pole is adorned with a jagged piece of the eye of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]]. The staff crackles with eldritch power. In the hands of a spellcaster they can over channel their spells to cast them with the Greater effect by spending 1 Charge. The Megaohm Stave has 2 charges that recharge on Settle In.

It is held by [[Reginald]].
