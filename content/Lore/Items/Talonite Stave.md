Ornate staff carved of [[Dantalion the Unseen]]'s talon. It's adorned with a carved talon clutching a sapphire. When a spell goes awry, the sapphire gleams and steadies the wild magic with owl's wisdom. You can spend it's charge to treat a 7-9 while casting a spell as a 10+. Recharges on Settle In.

It is held by [[Theodore Shimmerwing III]].
