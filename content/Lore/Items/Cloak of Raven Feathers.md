A ink-black cloak of raven feathers. As it's made from the feathers of the most eldritch bird it is resistant to magic and prevents scrying of any kind on the wearer. [[Flint Toothache]] traded this from [[Wheeler]] in [[Session 5 - Summer, Year 2]].

The heroes learned from [[Dantalion the Unseen]] in [[Session 7 - Winter, Year 2]] that this cloak hides the wearer from the piercing gaze of [[Lampades the All-Seeing]].

[[Flint Toothache]] used this cloak to help him slay [[Lampades the All-Seeing]], even reflected some his lightning.
