A magical liquid created from the sap from the [[Sacred Maple]] and blood of [[Tyrants]]. It is used to quench a blade of legendary quality as part of the rites of the [[Autumn King]].

[[Arthur No-Mane]] alongside his two sworn knights [[Alistair the Scarred]] and [[Peridot the Darting Blade]] brewed this on the Autumnal Equinox of [[Session 10 - Autumn, Year 3]].
