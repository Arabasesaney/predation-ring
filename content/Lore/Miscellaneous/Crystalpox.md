An affliction that causes iridescent blue crystal growth on the victim. The more the crystals grow, the more sick the victim becomes. It is spread by the crystals themselves ending up in water sources and drank by unknowing hosts. It causes the infected water to be slightly bluer than usual.

When Crystalpox is cured, the crystals turn clear like quartz, transmute to water, and fall of the skin.

This condition was first encountered in [[Session 1 - Spring]] in the [[Fortress of the Crystal Rat]].
