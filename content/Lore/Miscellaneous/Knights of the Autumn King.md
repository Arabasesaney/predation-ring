An ancient order lives only in stories told to [[Arthur No-Mane]] as a wet-eared youth. In fact, they did once exist.

The heroes learned of their former glory from [[Alistair]] in [[Session 4b - Spring, Year 2]].

It is said that to reforge the knights, a [[Sacred Samara]] must be planted and grow into a Sacred Maple. Then, a weapon of legendary quality must be quenched in it's sap. Lastly, on the Autumnal Equinox the name of the [[Autumn King]] must be spoken.
