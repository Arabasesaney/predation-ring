A tree with leaves more vibrant than any other, born from the [[Sacred Samara]]. This tree holds a special significance for the [[Knights of the Autumn King]].

[[Arthur No-Mane]] in [[Session 4b - Spring, Year 2]] dug up the [[Sacred Samara]] under the [[Autumnal Obelisk]]. He brought it back to [[Elmbridge]] and planted it there.

In [[Session 7 - Winter, Year 2]], at the end of that year it had already grown to be the size of a 5-year maple tree.
